Your Rights

With the recent implementation of General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), it gives people a certain rights towards their personal data being collected by us. As such, we recognise and therefore comply to GDPR and the rights as per below :-

Right to be forgotten

Other than our service-related messages, you have the rights to opt-out any communications from us or our partners. The opt-outs will be done within 10 days after the request is being made. You can also request your personal data to be erased from our database in situations of information being not relevant anymore or you would like to stop using our service completely.

Right to access

You have the rights to ask whether a certain information about yourself is being processed, where is it being stored and for what purpose. You can also request for us to provide to you a copy of your personal data that is being stored by us.

Data portability

You have the rights to use the data being stored by us to be extracted and shared to other services of your choice.

Breach of notification

We are responsible to report any data breaches to you within 72 hours after we were made aware of the breach.

Privacy by Design

Our systems are to be designed to only obtain relevant information and to process only the necessary data needed to complete our duties.

Data Protection Officer (DPO)

DPOs are only required if the company is processing and monitoring large amount of data on a regular basis or specific categories of data and data relating to criminal convictions and offences of which in this case we do not and only process data when necessary.

Majority of these rights are able to be exercised and updated once you have signed into your account. If you have any questions or in need of assistance, please contact us at support@mybillone.com.

# Transactional Email

## Personalise your digital delivery and attachment with just the click of a few buttons.

Transactional emails allow you to provide information that is uniquely relevant to the recipient. These emails can provide you with a requested attachment which are password protected.

myBillOne allows an organization to create transactional email templates which can then be sent to most a list of your contact safely and securely.

myBillOne brings you a unique cloud based digital delivery and archiving platform that enables your business to better connect with an individual organization’s customer using its unique personalization feature.

Seamless delivery of bills, statements, marketing promos.
